# Arcanist
Arcanists are those who seek and use knowledge of the arcane.  Their magic is a not a matter of faith (like the Cleric), but rather that of study and formulae.  Arcanists are the worst of all the classes at fighting; hours spent studying massive tomes of magic do not lead a character to become strong or adept with weapons.  They are the least hardy, having the least Hit Points and worst Saving Throws.

## Features
- **Prime Requisite:** INT 11+
- **Starting HP**: 4 + CON Modifier
- **Hit Dice (levels 2 to 10):** 1d4 + CON Modifier
- **HP for Levels 11+:** +1 per Level
- **Armor:** None
- **Weapons:** Small & Staff

## Equipment
- 1d6sp
- An Adventurer's Pack
- A Mage's Pack

## Arcanist Skills
**Detect Magic (+0):** Being sensitive to the world of magic, an arcanist may determine if a person, place or thing is magical. Doing so requires [[Concentration]] and a full minute; so you cannot tell if something is magical simply by being in its presence and people tend to notice when a spellcaster is staring at them. You can also attempt to decipher magical writing in the same way with this skill.

**Lore (-2):** Arcanists are well studied in the areas of magic and mysticism.  This skill may be used to identify creatures, magical artifacts and other information relating to magic and the arcane.  The more the roll exceeds the target [Difficulty](CoreRules.md#Difficulty), the more details the character knows.

**Prepare Scroll (+0):** arcanists can create arcane spell scrolls and protection scrolls.  See the rules on [magic item creation]() for details.

**Brew Potion (-1):** arcanists can brew potions.  See the rules on [magic item creation]() for details.

**Create Minor Magic Item (-3):** Starting at 5th level, arcanists can create minor arcane items.  See the rules on [magic item creation]() for details.

**Create Major Magic Item (-5):** Starting at 10th level, arcanists can create major arcane items.  See the rules on [magic item creation]() for details.

**Create Magic Artifact (-10):** Starting at 14th level, arcanists can create arcane artifacts.  See the rules on [magic item creation]() for details.

*The DM should typically roll for Detect Magic and Brew Potion as it may not be immediately obvious if the skill check succeeds or not.*

## Arcane Magic
**Magical Focus:** As part of their apprenticeship, arcanists create a Magical Focus to aid in casting spells. This focus may take most any form (a staff, wand, amulet or similar), but must be easily seen, recognized and hand-held. It also takes up 1 [Encumbrance Slot](EncumbranceAndEquipment.md#Encumbrance). A focus may be replaced by constructing a new one which takes 1 week in a [Safe Haven](Rest_and_Recovery.md#Safe%20Haven) and 100sp worth of materials.

For every level the arcanist has, they may carve a *sigil* into their focus.  Each sigil represents a specific [Spell](magic/Spells.md) the arcanist has learned (i.e. recorded in their [Spellbook](magic/MagicalArts.md#Spellbooks)). Whenever they cast one of these spells, while holding their **Magical Focus**, the player rolls d8s for their [Invocation Roll](magic/MagicalArts.md#The%20Invocation%20Roll) instead of d6s. It takes 1 Day times the spell's [Tier](magic/Spells.md#Tier) and 50sp per Day to engrave a new sigil into a focus.

Having a **Magical Focus** in hand does not count against needing a free hand for spell casting.

**Invocation Dice:** As spellcasters, arcanists have the number of [Invocation Dice](magic/MagicalArts.md#The%20Invocation%20Dice%20Pool) indicated on the table below, plus a number of additional dice equal to their [Intelligence Modifier](CharacterCreation.md#Attribute%20Modifiers).  The arcanist may choose how many dice to roll each time they attempt to cast a spell.

**Spellcasting:** arcanists record their spells in their [Spellbooks](magic/MagicalArts.md#Spellbooks) as arcane formulae.  Before a spell can be cast, however, it must be prepared.  The table below shows the number of spells of each level they may prepare after a full night's rest.  This takes 10 minutes of uninterrupted meditation and ritual times the [Tier](magic/Spells.md#Tier) of the spell.

**Exhausting Magic:** At a cost of 1 level of [Exhaustion](Conditions.md#Exhaustion), a arcanist may choose to pass an invocation roll that just failed.  This occurs after a normal [Invocation Roll](magic/MagicalArts.md#The%20Invocation%20Roll), and any expended *Invocation Dice* are still expended and any [Mishaps](magic/MagicalArts.md#Mishaps) still occur.

**Tradition:** An arcanist must select an Arcane Tradition (Wizardry, Sorcery, Necromancy, etc).  This will determine which spells they have access to.  See the rules on [Magic](magic/MagicalArts.md) for details.
